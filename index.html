Health Care Engineering Systems Center, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

GazeAgent: Query-Driven Gaze Analysis

Project Lead
  • Conceived and led GazeAgent to bridge spatial gaze predictions and behavioral analysis, enabling natural-language questions about attention targets, durations, and event sequences in naturalistic interactions.
  • Designed a semantic gaze-event representation that grounds frame-level predictions in tracked scene entities, capturing object attention, eye contact, and gaze shifts.
  • Built a code-generating agent that executes queries as Python analyses over the event timeline, separating perception from analysis for inspection and re-execution.
  • Developed a 125-query benchmark and ran a controlled user study (N = 16): 83.3% temporal-context accuracy vs. 66.7% for the strongest video-language baseline, with reduced workload (NASA-TLX, p = .001).

GazeAnywhere

CVPR 2026 · Equal Contribution
  • Developed a data engine extending gaze datasets with text prompts identifying whose gaze to estimate, combining cross-dataset annotation alignment, VLM-based pseudo-annotation, and automated verification with human review.
  • Built Gaze-Co from GazeFollow, VideoAttentionTarget, and ChildPlay: ~120K prompt-annotated training samples plus concept-based test sets for promptable gaze target estimation.
  • Fine-tuned open-vocabulary detection to supply text-conditioned head boxes to conventional gaze baselines; evaluated adapted pipelines and ran ablations.
